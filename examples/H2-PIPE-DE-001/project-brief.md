# Project Brief — GreenArtery H2 Transmission Pipeline

## Scope

500 km onshore high-pressure hydrogen transmission pipeline, DN400, MAOP 100 bar,
connecting the Wilhelmshaven import terminal to the industrial cluster in the
Hamburg/Ruhr corridor (Germany). Four compression stations at km 0 (origination),
150, 300, and 450. Cathodic protection, full SCADA integration, 12 block valve
stations.

Key parameters:
- Pipeline length: 500 km
- Diameter: DN400
- Operating pressure: 100 bar MAOP
- Compression stations: 4 × reciprocating compressor, H2-rated dry gas seals
- Approx. 18 river crossings, 240 road crossings, 6 railway crossings
